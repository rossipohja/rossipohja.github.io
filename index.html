A private investment company based in Helsinki, Finland. We invest our own capital, on our own timeline.

What we do

Venture funding.

We back early-stage growth companies and the founders building them. Rossipohja was established in 2016 as the investment vehicle of two entrepreneurs who have built businesses themselves and know what the early years actually look like from the inside.

We invest our own capital. No fund life cycle, no investment committee, no pressure to exit on someone else's schedule. When we believe in a team, we can decide quickly and stay for as long as it takes.

We do not seek control. What we offer beyond capital is a long memory, a Nordic network, and a straight answer.
